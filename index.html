Academics

Learning pathways designed for every stage of growth.

KG

  • Curriculum: Activity-based early learning
  • Methodology: Play-based exploration and literacy immersion
  • Assessment: Observation and developmental checkpoints
  • Co-curricular: Music, art, movement and storytelling
  • Outcomes: Confidence, curiosity, social readiness

Primary School

  • Curriculum: Foundational literacy, numeracy, science and values
  • Methodology: Experiential learning and small-group mentoring
  • Assessment: Formative assessments and classroom projects
  • Co-curricular: Clubs, public speaking and art appreciation
  • Outcomes: Concept clarity, communication and teamwork

Middle School

  • Curriculum: Integrated academic and project-led learning
  • Methodology: Inquiry-based teaching and digital classroom tools
  • Assessment: Continuous evaluations and competency checks
  • Co-curricular: Robotics, sports, leadership initiatives
  • Outcomes: Critical thinking and academic resilience

High School

  • Curriculum: Board-aligned academic stream and enrichment modules
  • Methodology: Mentorship, challenge-based learning and revision planning
  • Assessment: Unit tests, practical exams and portfolio tasks
  • Co-curricular: Science fairs, debates and extracurricular clubs
  • Outcomes: Academic mastery and future preparedness

Higher Secondary

  • Curriculum: Science, commerce and humanities streams
  • Methodology: Result-focused planning and career mentoring
  • Assessment: Board practice, mock exams and intensive reviews
  • Co-curricular: Internships, leadership and competitive exam support
  • Outcomes: University readiness and career clarity
